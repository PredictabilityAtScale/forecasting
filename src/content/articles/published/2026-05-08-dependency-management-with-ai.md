# Dependency Management with AI

PDF: [Dependency Management with AI in Flight Levels Systems](/presentations/Dependency%20Management%20with%20AI%20in%20Flight%20Levels%20Systems.pdf)

I recently gave a presentation on dependency management with AI. It covers my current thinking about how AI changes dependency management, especially when work spans multiple teams, systems, and decision levels.

The short version: AI can help expose hidden dependencies earlier, but it does not remove the need for clear ownership, explicit sequencing, and fast feedback loops. The biggest opportunity is not simply automating dependency tracking. It is using AI to make weak signals easier to see before they become delivery surprises.

The presentation walks through how dependency management changes when AI is part of the delivery system, where it can help, and where leaders still need deliberate operating agreements.
